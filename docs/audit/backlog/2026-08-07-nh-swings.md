**Date:** 2026-08-07

**Symptom** — A member reads the actionable swing board and sees, e.g., `CRWV 84C · 3DTE ·

**Evidence** (poll 13:39:31Z; DTE recomputed against 2026-08-07):

`DTE drift == days since firstSeenAt` for **every** row whose `signalKinds == ["NIGHT HAWK"]`
(9/9). The one non-legacy row (BE, `["CATALYST","FLOW","STRUCTURE"]`) has drift 0. Served

implies spot ≈ $373 (`flagUnderlyingPx` 373.51) — live spot is $355.58. AEM 160C at $9.38
implies ≈ $163 (`flagUnderlyingPx` 163.25) — live spot is $180.21, where that call's intrinsic

Frozen INTRA-session too — identical mids across all three polls (13:39Z / ~13:47Z / 13:58Z):
CRWV 5.31 / 5.31 / 5.31, SKHY 6.44 / 6.44 / 6.44, GOOGL 17.36 ×3, AEM 9.38 ×3, LLY 28.99 ×3.
(By contrast the five MANAGING rows DO re-mark: KRE 1.13 → 0.98, SPCX 11.05 → 8.18.)

Lane-contract violation on top of the price error: `HORIZONS.SWING` is **5–30 DTE**
(`src/lib/horizons.ts:32`). At serve time CRWV/SKHY/RDDT are 0 DTE and AMD/NVDA are 3 DTE —
**6 of 10 pre-entry rows are below the lane's own DTE floor**, three of them expiring within
the hour of the audit.

**Root cause** — `src/lib/swing/legacy-confirm-promote.ts:327-341`
`carryLegacyPromotedIntoSnapshot(fresh, prior)`: after each `swing-discovery` scan overwrites
the serving snapshot, every prior triple whose watch row `isLegacyPromotedSignal(signalKinds)`

re-attached from the prior one verbatim** (`stripTickersFromSnapshot` → `mergeLegacyPromotedSnapshot`).

`spotsByTicker` are refreshed (`src/app/api/cron/swing-discovery/route.ts:355`). The fresh scan
itself is fine — it stamps `asOfYmd: deps.sessionDay` (`src/lib/swing/discovery.ts:651`), which
is why the one natively-discovered row (BE) has zero drift.

**Suggested fix** — on carry, re-resolve the contract for the carried thesis against today's
chain (re-run the sub-lane contract ranker, or at minimum re-price the same OCC and recompute
DTE from `sessionDay`), and DROP the carried row when the re-resolved contract falls outside
`HORIZONS.SWING.dteMin/dteMax` instead of serving an expiring one. Deliberately leave alone:
the carry mechanism itself (morning-confirm handoff is intentional) and the persistence gate.
