## 2026-09-03 — [FINDING, P3 Correctness] Night Hawk `factor_breakdown` still omitted 3 of `dossier_score`'s inputs (iv/anomaly/flow-conviction) + the regime multiplier's net effect — FIXED

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What prompted this** | Operator-requested close audit of all live plays across SPX Slayer, Night Hawk 0DTE, Legacy, and Vector. The audit re-examined the "Why this play was picked" panel (`factor_breakdown`) after the 2026-09-02 fix (task #70 / PR history) that widened it from 5 to 11 named component scores, and found it was *still* not reconciling to `dossier_score` on some rows. |
| **Root cause** | `scoreCandidate` (`src/features/nighthawk/lib/scorer.ts`) folds 3 more terms into `total`/`score` beyond the 11 already-surfaced components — `ivAdjustment` (IV-rank options-pricing penalty), `anomalyPenalty` (-10 flow-anomaly demotion), `flowConvictionBonus` (+4 for very strong directional flow) — and then applies a `dampenedRegime` multiplier (0.6-1.3x range, dampened by 0.5) to the whole sum. None of the 11-component fix touched these: they were computed locally inside `scoreCandidate` and never attached to the returned `ScoredCandidate` object at all, so no downstream consumer (board.ts's `enrichSetup`, deterministic-edition.ts's two `factor_breakdown` builders) could ever have rendered them even after being widened. Measured live 2026-09-03: an NVDA case with a nonzero `flowConvictionBonus` showed `dossier_score=81` against a `factor_breakdown` sum of `77` — the missing 4 points were exactly the bonus. |
| **Fix** | `scorer.ts`: expose `iv_adjustment`, `anomaly_penalty`, `flow_conviction_bonus` on `ScoredCandidate` (conditional — only when non-zero, matching the file's existing "absent vs real zero" convention for every other optional dimension), plus a new `regime_adjustment` field that captures the multiplier's own net point delta (`total - round(preRegimeSum)`) so a breakdown that includes all raw components still reconciles exactly to `total` even when the regime multiplier isn't neutral. Wired all four into the three `factor_breakdown` builders that already mirror `ScoredCandidate`'s shape: `board.ts`'s `enrichSetup` (FLOW/PIN setups) and both of `deterministic-edition.ts`'s builders (Night Hawk Edition plays). |
| **Blast radius** | Same three call sites the 2026-09-02 fix (task #70) touched — this is the second half of the same gap, not a new surface. No scoring/gating logic changed; `total`/`score`/`dossier_score` are numerically identical before and after (the fix only exposes numbers that were already being computed and summed, it doesn't change what gets summed). |
| **What was deliberately left unchanged** | The scoring formula itself (weights, caps, the dampening factor) — this is a display/traceability fix, not a scoring change. `regime_adjustment` is a derived delta, not a first-class scored dimension, so it's presented as a synthetic entry rather than trying to distribute the multiplier proportionally across the other 14 components (which would misrepresent which component the regime actually penalized/rewarded — the multiplier is genuinely global, not per-component). |
| **Regression guard** | `src/lib/zerodte/board.test.ts`: new test `enrich: factor_breakdown carries iv/anomaly/flow-conviction/regime adjustments and sums to dossier_score` — constructs a `ScoredCandidate` with all three new fields set, asserts they render under their own keys, asserts a real-zero field (`anomaly_penalty` absent when 0) stays omitted per the existing convention, and asserts `Object.values(factor_breakdown).reduce(sum) === dossier_score`. |
| **Gates** | `npx tsc --noEmit` clean (Node 20.20.2). `src/lib/zerodte/board.test.ts` 129/129 pass. `src/features/nighthawk/lib/deterministic-edition.test.ts` 41/41 pass. Full `npm test` run in progress at time of writing — will report if any unrelated failure surfaces. |
| **Status** | FIXED |
