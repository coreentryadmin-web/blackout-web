## 2026-09-04 — [FINDING, P2 correctness, Largo morning brief] `largo-morning-brief` had no ET gate on its dual-band cron schedule — every opted-in member got the push TWICE daily, one of them a full hour off — FIXED

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Status** | FIXED |
| **What** | `GET /api/cron/largo-morning-brief` (member-facing push, `produces_member_alert: true` in `cron-registry.ts`) is registered on EventBridge with `schedule_cron_utc: "25 13,14 * * 1-5"` — a **dual-band** fixed-UTC schedule that fires at **both** 13:25 UTC and 14:25 UTC, every weekday, year-round. That pattern only ever makes sense as "cover the same ET instant across both DST regimes" (see `nighthawk-morning-confirm`'s identical `"15 13,14 * * 1-5"`) — but this route had **no in-app ET-window gate at all**. It ran its full pipeline — build the brief, then `SELECT` every opted-in member and send a real web push — unconditionally on **every** invocation. |
| **Root cause** | `13:25 UTC` = 9:25 ET under EDT (summer) but 8:25 ET under EST (winter); `14:25 UTC` = 10:25 ET under EDT but 9:25 ET under EST. Exactly one of the two fires lands on the intended 9:25 ET open in any given season — the other is a full hour off. The route's comment (`/** 9:25 ET weekday morning brief */`) and the registry's `schedule_label: "9:25 AM ET weekdays"` both assert a single daily 9:25 ET fire, but nothing in the code enforced it: `route.ts` went straight from the `isCronAuthorized` check into `buildLargoMorningBrief()` + the push loop, with zero reference to the current ET time. So the route ran the full pipeline **twice every single weekday, all year** — not only during the week of a DST transition — and one of those two runs landed at the wrong ET hour. |
| **Evidence** | `src/lib/cron-registry.ts:68` — `schedule_cron_utc: "25 13,14 * * 1-5"` with the comment "Mirrors railway.largo-morning-brief.toml dual-band — off-window stale suppression (ops #2565, #2569)": the dual-band was added purely to satisfy `cron-staleness-watchdog`, not paired with a route-side gate the way its sibling `nighthawk-morning-confirm` is (`inMorningWindow(force)` → `inEtWindow({ targetHour: 9, targetMinute: 10, catchupMin: 35 })`, see that route's own header comment: "dual band so one firing always lands in the 9:10-9:45 ET window across both DST regimes"). Read `src/app/api/cron/largo-morning-brief/route.ts` pre-fix in full — no `inEtWindow`, no ET check of any kind, no `?force=1`, nothing between the auth check and `buildLargoMorningBrief()`. New regression test `src/app/api/cron/largo-morning-brief/route.test.ts` fails RED against the pre-fix route (`git stash` proof, ran with `node --import tsx --experimental-test-module-mocks --test`): mocking `inEtWindow` to return `false` had zero effect — `buildLargoMorningBrief` still ran (`buildCalls` stayed `1` instead of the expected `0`) because the pre-fix route never imported or called it at all. |
| **Blast radius** | Only this one route — `largo-morning-brief`. Its sibling `nighthawk-morning-confirm` uses the same dual-band schedule shape but already has the `inEtWindow` gate (confirmed by reading its `route.ts`), so it is unaffected. `nighthawk-outcomes` (`"30 20,21 * * 1-5"`) is a separate DB-write cron, not a push — checked its route and it is idempotent per-row (first-write-wins on the outcome), so a double-fire there is harmless in a way a push notification never can be; left untouched, in scope for a different lane if it ever needs the same treatment. No other cron in `cron-registry.ts` carries `produces_member_alert: true` alongside a comma-listed multi-hour `schedule_cron_utc` with no matching in-route gate — spot-checked `nighthawk-outcomes`, `gex-eod-snapshot`, `zerodte-grade`, `spx-signal-weight-optimize` (none are member push routes; the first two are already documented in `CLAUDE.md`'s cron-dst-audit section as correctly idempotent/gated). |
| **Fix rationale** | Mirrored the exact pattern already shipped and proven correct for `nighthawk-morning-confirm`: a private `inMorningWindow(force)` helper backed by the shared, unit-tested `inEtWindow` primitive (`src/features/nighthawk/lib/et-window.ts`), gating right after the auth check and before any work (DB read, brief build, or push) happens. Target window is `{ targetHour: 9, targetMinute: 25, catchupMin: 30 }` (9:25-9:55 ET) — wide enough to absorb ECS/EventBridge scheduling jitter, narrow enough that the OTHER dual-band fire (a full hour off in either direction) always lands outside it. `?force=1` bypasses the gate for manual/agent-driven runs, matching every other gated cron in this codebase. Off-window fires now return `{ ok: true, skipped: true, reason: ... }` and still call `logCronRun` (so cron-health/staleness suppression, the entire reason the dual-band schedule exists, keeps working) — deliberately did **not** touch `cron-registry.ts` or the EventBridge schedule itself, since the dual-band UTC schedule is still the correct/only way to hit 9:25 ET in both DST regimes; the bug was purely the missing in-app filter on which of the two fires should do real work. |
| **Test** | `src/app/api/cron/largo-morning-brief/route.test.ts` — 3 cases: off-window is a no-op (brief never built, no push sent, `skipped: true` logged), in-window runs normally (brief built exactly once), `?force=1` bypasses the gate. RED pre-fix (`git stash` on `route.ts` alone), GREEN post-fix; `npx tsc --noEmit` clean. |
