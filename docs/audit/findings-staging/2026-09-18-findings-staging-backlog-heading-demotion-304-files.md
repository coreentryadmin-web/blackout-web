| **What was done** | Read-only classification of all 342 currently-staged files against the exact `normalizeEntry()` heading/kind-line logic, then a conservative demotion: files where every non-first `## ` line matches a curated whitelist of known process-structure labels (Symptom, Root cause, Fix, Evidence, Blast radius, RTH validation, Tests, Status, Regression, Market-open validation, etc.) AND none carry a bundled-finding signal (a `(#N)` bug-reference suffix, a `Bug N` prefix, or a `YYYY-MM-DD — [...]`-style dated sub-heading) get every `## ` after the first demoted to `### ` (a lead `# H1` title is promoted to `## ` where that was the file's only true heading). 304 of 342 files matched. PR: `fix/findings-staging-subsection-heading-demotion`. |
