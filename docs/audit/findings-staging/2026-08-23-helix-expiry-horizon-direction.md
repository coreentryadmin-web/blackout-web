## 2026-08-23 — [P2, Helix] Every Expiry Concentration bar rendered bullish green, and all four disagree with the direction rule the rest of the page uses — FIXED

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Root cause** | `ExpiryConcentration` coloured each horizon bar from option type alone: `callPct = callPremium / (callPremium + putPremium)`, then `isBull = callPct >= 55`. Calls dominate a bucket → green bar, green premium figure. But **a SOLD call is bearish**, and #2691 replaced exactly this rule everywhere else on `/flows` (`flowDirection`, `DIRECTION_BASIS = "aggression_aware_v1"`). The tide bar above this panel and the split-flow radar below it both read the aggression-aware rule; the panel between them read the one they had moved off. Two components on one page can paint opposite colours from the same tape. |
| **Evidence — measured, not argued** | Live production tape, 5000 rows / 168h, 2026-08-23, via the new `scripts/audit/helix-expiry-direction-probe.mjs`. **All four horizons rendered BULLISH GREEN. All four disagree with the shipped rule.**<br><br>`0DTE` — $149.6M, 63.0% readable → **mixed**; 42.7% of readable call premium was SOLD.<br>`This week` — $62.1M, 84.7% readable → **mixed**; **bearish premium $26,302,085 slightly EXCEEDS bullish $26,231,879**, under a green bar. 50.7% of readable call premium SOLD.<br>`Monthly` — $2.17B, **6.1% readable** → **undetermined**; of what can be read, bearish $74.3M exceeds bullish $59.2M. 59.2% of readable call premium SOLD.<br>`LEAPS` — $8.46B, **3.2% readable** → **undetermined**; $8,188,655,250 of $8,458,709,691 has no readable direction at all. |
| **The second defect, which is the larger one** | `ask_pct` is a Group A field (HELIX-MAP §4A); Monthly and LEAPS are dominated by the SPX/SPY index feed, which does not carry it. So the direction of **94% of Monthly premium and 97% of LEAPS premium is not readable**, and the panel painted a confident green over it. That is worse than a wrong colour: to a member it is indistinguishable from an evidenced one. Absence rendered as a verdict — `_COMMON.md` #7, on the largest premium on the page. |
| **Fix** | `src/features/helix/lib/helix-expiry-horizon.ts`. `horizonDirection(flows)` runs the SHIPPED `directionalPremium` + `directionLabel` over the bucket's own prints and returns the verdict **together with `readablePct`** — the share of the horizon's premium the verdict could be drawn from. `horizonTone` returns `null` (neutral) whenever `readablePct < 50`, whatever the readable slice leans. The panel colours from that, and renders `direction unread · N% sided` in amber whenever the read is withheld, so **a neutral bar is legible as "could not tell" rather than "balanced"** — the distinction the fix exists to preserve. `horizonDirectionTitle` states the basis, the split, the unreadable dollars, and the four-way rule itself, so a member can check the colour against the numbers instead of trusting it. |
| **Fix rationale — why `readablePct` is part of the verdict, not a diagnostic** | `directionLabel` already refuses when unreadable premium exceeds readable, so the *label* was safe on its own. The **colour** was not, and neither was the member's read of a neutral bar. Returning the share the refusal was made from is what lets the surface distinguish its two neutral cases. Expressing the gate as a share rather than a second threshold keeps the two rules from ever disagreeing about a bucket: at exactly 50/50 they agree, and below it both refuse. A test pins that seam from both sides. |
| **What was deliberately NOT changed** | `callPremium` / `putPremium` stay on the bucket and stay in the payload. They are this panel's own native fact, and the Largo product contract is **ADDITIVE** — flattening a product's own intelligence to satisfy a shared shape is a violation, not compliance. They simply no longer decide the colour. The `bucketLabel` / `bucketMaxTotal` / `barWidthPct` logic and its tests are untouched; this changes what a bar MEANS, not how long it is. |
| **A latent fabrication fixed on the way past** | `callPct` fell back to **`50`** when total premium was 0 — a fabricated 50/50 balance, the same defect as the tide bar's flat bar (#2704). It is unreachable today because the bucket list is filtered at a $50k floor, so this is stated as latent rather than as a live defect. It is gone regardless: the replacement returns `readablePct: null` for an empty horizon, never `0`, because `0%` would claim "we measured and none of it was readable" where nothing was measured. |
| **Checked and found NOT to be a defect** | `cur.count++` runs for every print while premium is added only for `option_type === "CALL" \| "PUT"`, so a third type would make "N prints" cover a wider population than the premium beside it. Measured: **5000/5000 rows are CALL or PUT** (3025 / 1975), zero others. The two figures do describe the same population. Recorded because "I checked and it does not happen" is a different, and more useful, statement than not having looked. |
| **Blast radius** | `ExpiryConcentration` renders on `/flows` only. `helix-flow-aggression.ts` is imported, not modified, so #2691's rule and its ledger `direction_basis` stamp are untouched — this panel joins that rule rather than forking it. No API, no payload, no persisted field changes; the direction is derived client-side from prints the panel already had in hand. |
| **New harness** | `scripts/audit/helix-expiry-direction-probe.mjs` — read-only against prod, one temp Clerk user deleted in a `finally`, imports the REAL `flowDirection` / `directionalPremium` / `bucketLabel` rather than restating them. Reports legacy colour vs shipped verdict, readable share, and the share of readable CALL premium that was SOLD, per horizon. **Exits non-zero on any disagreement**, so post-deploy it becomes the check that the fix is actually live. Prints `INSUFFICIENT DATA` on an empty tape rather than four clean horizons — "nothing to measure" and "measured, all fine" are opposite results and must not share an output. |
| **Regression guard** | `helix-expiry-horizon.test.ts`, 9 tests: a horizon of sold calls reading **bearish** (the defect stated as a test — 100% call premium, formerly green); bought calls still bullish, so the fix does not invert the common case; the live LEAPS shape (a lopsidedly bullish readable slice under $8.2B of unreadable premium) yielding **no colour**; the readable share reported even at 1%; an empty horizon reporting `null` rather than `0%`; the 50/50 seam agreeing with `directionLabel`'s own refusal from both sides; genuinely two-sided premium reading **mixed** rather than rounding to a side (the live "This week" shape); 90%-call-premium-all-sold reading bearish — the single assertion that the panel changed RULE and not threshold; and the tooltip carrying basis, split and coverage all three. |
