## 2026-09-02 — [P2/P3, marketing copy accuracy] Homepage "one membership"/"one price" claims and the 5-minute onboarding FAQ contradicted the two-tier pricing section — FIXED

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Value |

| **Reported by** | User, fresh production review, three related items in one report. |
| **Item 1 — "one membership" overclaim** | The homepage's "Built like a trading desk" comparison list said "{productCount} products, one platform, one membership," but the pricing section on the SAME page immediately offers a separate $49/mo SPX Slayer membership alongside the $199/mo Full Desk membership — "one membership" is false as a platform-wide claim. Confirmed by reading `RedesignHome.tsx`'s own pricing grid (lines ~447-470 SPX Slayer, ~473+ Premium/Full Desk) and `RedesignPricing.tsx`'s `PLAN_MATRIX` — genuinely two separate memberships exist. Notably, this exact bullet was ALREADY corrected once today from an even worse claim ("one screen") per `RedesignHome.seo.test.ts`'s existing regression comment — the first fix ("one platform, one membership") still overclaimed the membership count, just less severely. Fixed to "{productCount} products, one unified Full Desk" — true of every product without asserting there is only one membership tier. |
| **Item 2 — "no add-ons, no upsells... one price" overclaim** | The products section said "No add-ons, no upsells: the whole desk is one price," which reads as a single site-wide price point — again contradicted by the two-tier pricing section on the same page. User's suggested rewording ("Full Desk includes every product—no per-product add-ons") is accurate: it describes the Full Desk plan's own bundling, not a site-wide single price. Applied verbatim (with an em dash for house style). |
| **Item 3 — onboarding FAQ assumed everyone's first stop is SPX Slayer** | `src/lib/faq/content.ts`'s "How do I get started in 5 minutes?" answer unconditionally said "unlock Premium, and open the SPX Slayer desk" regardless of what kind of trader is reading it — a flow trader, an earnings trader, or a market-wide scanner user would all be routed to the same SPX-specific desk. Rewritten to route by trading style (flow → HELIX, earnings → Meridian, market-wide scanning → Vector, SPX/SPXW 0DTE → SPX Slayer), noting the non-SPX-Slayer desks require Premium (since SPX Slayer alone doesn't include them, per Item 1/2's own two-tier reality) — then the same "ask Largo" / "Learn layer" close as before. |
| **What was deliberately left unchanged** | `RedesignPricing.tsx`'s "Every product · one membership" and `RedesignHome.tsx`'s pricing-card "Every product — one membership" are both scoped specifically to the Premium/Full Desk plan card they sit inside — true of that plan, not a platform-wide claim, so not touched. The FAQ's own "What's included in Premium?" answer ("The entire arsenal, one membership...") is similarly plan-scoped and correct as-is. |
| **AI verification claim — investigated, not yet resolved this cycle** | The user also flagged "AI verification engine gates every play" as possibly stronger than reality and asked whether literally every graded play across every play-generating product passes the same BIE/Cortex stack. Not fixed this cycle: narrowing the claim correctly requires confirming SPX Slayer's actual gating mechanism and how "verification" should be worded to stay accurate without wrongly implying SPX Slayer has NO verification at all (it likely has its own, just not BIE/Cortex) — a mischaracterization in the other direction would be an equally real bug. Flagging for a follow-up pass rather than shipping a rushed, possibly-wrong narrowing. |
