## 2026-09-21 — [FINDING, P3 Largo/Night Hawk Swings] Swing play-brief `dataFreshnessSection`/`collectBriefUnavailableSources` still sampled the real wall clock instead of `ctx.readMs` — the exact defect shape #5351 fixed for a different section pair — FIXED

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Symptom** | Same-day follow-up to #5351 (`fix/swing-play-brief-staleness-anchor`), which added `SwingPlayBriefContext.readMs` — a single canonical "now" `composeSwingPlayBrief` stamps once before any section builds — and wired it into the two call sites (`watchForSection`, `confluenceCoaching`) that a live SNXX repro caught disagreeing on the same put wall. That fix deliberately left ~18 other `Date.now()` staleness call sites across `play-brief*.ts` untouched as a documented follow-up sweep. Two of those sites are the highest-risk of the remaining eighteen because they sit directly opposite sections that already were anchored: `dataFreshnessSection` (play-brief-intel.ts) narrates "Option mark **stale**" / "Vector data Xs old" / "GEX matrix Xs old" for the SAME fields `collectBriefUnavailableSources` (play-brief-absence.ts) turns into the UI's `unavailableSources[]` / `UnavailableChip` — and neither had ever consulted `ctx.readMs`, even though both already receive `ctx` and both are called from the same `composeSwingPlayBrief` request. A member could see a "stale" chip and a non-qualified "as of" narrative line for the identical `markAsOf`, or vice versa, purely because compose's real sequential I/O (Meridian, book-context, archetype/ticker track-record reads) let the two functions' own `Date.now()` samples land on opposite sides of the 18-minute `SWING_OPTION_MARK_STALE_MS` / 120s `VECTOR_STALE_MS` / `GEX_MATRIX_STALE_MS` cutoffs. |
| **Root cause** | `dataFreshnessSection` called `optionMarkIsStale(play, Date.now())`, `vectorAgeStale(vec, Date.now())`, and `gexMatrixStale(ctx.ecosystem?.gex_positioning, Date.now())` — three independent real-clock samples in one section. `collectBriefUnavailableSources` called `collectOptionMarkStalenessAbsence(ctx.play, Date.now())`, `collectGexStalenessAbsence(gex, Date.now())`, `collectVectorStalenessAbsence(vec, ctx.sessionDate)` (implicit `Date.now()` default), `meridianCatalystStale(ctx.meridian, Date.now())`, and `newsCatalystStale(ctx.ecosystem.arsenal.news.as_of, Date.now())` — five more, in the function that builds the chips a member sees alongside every narrative section. Every one of the underlying helpers (`optionMarkIsStale`, `vectorAgeStale`, `gexMatrixStale`, `gexMatrixAgeMs`, `collectOptionMarkStalenessAbsence`, `collectGexStalenessAbsence`, `collectVectorStalenessAbsence`, `meridianCatalystStale`, `newsCatalystStale`) already accepted an optional `readMs` parameter defaulting to `Date.now()` — the wiring gap was purely at these two call sites never passing it through. |
| **Why this wasn't just re-running #5351's own fix** | #5351 scoped itself deliberately narrow (the two sites its live SNXX repro actually exercised) and named the rest as a documented follow-up rather than guessing which of the remaining ~18 were worth fixing together. This PR picks the two highest-value ones: the pair that sits directly against `watchForSection` (already fixed) and each other, where a chip-vs-narrative disagreement is the most visible and most confusing failure mode (a member sees a red "stale" chip next to a green "as of" line for the same fact). |
| **Evidence** | Regression test (`dataFreshnessSection: uses ctx.readMs as the staleness anchor, not the real wall clock`, `play-brief-intel.test.ts`) anchors `ctx.readMs` one year in the future (past every staleness window) against a `markAsOf` that is fresh under the REAL wall clock at test-run time — proving the section must consult `ctx.readMs` rather than silently falling back. RED confirmed pre-fix (`must read the mark as stale under ctx.readMs even though it is fresh under the real wall clock` — assertion failed, section rendered the non-stale line). GREEN post-fix. Full suite: `npx tsx --experimental-test-module-mocks --test` across the four swing play-brief test files: 474/474 pass. `tsc --noEmit` clean. |
| **Fix** | `dataFreshnessSection` now samples `const readMs = ctx.readMs ?? Date.now();` once at the top and passes it to all three staleness calls (plus `gexMatrixAgeMs`, for the age label). `collectBriefUnavailableSources` does the identical `const readMs = ctx.readMs ?? Date.now();` at its top and threads it through all five remaining call sites listed above. |
| **Deliberately scoped down** | The other ~16 `Date.now()` staleness call sites across `play-brief.ts`/`play-brief-narrative.ts`/`play-brief-narrative-coaching.ts` (e.g. `wallDynamicsSection`, `vectorDeskSection`, `evidenceFromContext`'s already-`readMs`-parameterized-but-worth-double-checking helpers, various coaching bullets) are the same latent-risk shape and remain a follow-up — this PR again picks the two highest-value, most-directly-opposed sites rather than doing a single wide mechanical sweep, matching #5351's own stated scoping rationale. |
| **Not a trading-behaviour change** | No gate, score, rail, exit rule or grading path touched — only which wall-clock instant a freshness narrative/chip uses. |
| **Status** | FIXED. |
