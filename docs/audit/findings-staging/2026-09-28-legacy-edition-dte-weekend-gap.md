## 2026-09-28 — [FINDING, P2 Night Hawk Legacy] Legacy edition's contract selection and displayed `dte` anchor on the BUILD-time date, not the edition's target trading day — a Friday-built Monday edition can silently pick a contract that is effectively 0-DTE by the time a member reads it

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Symptom** | Live `GET /api/market/nighthawk/edition` (read 2026-09-28, ~02:40 ET, market not yet open): the edition is `edition_for: "2026-09-28"` (Monday), `published_at: "2026-09-25T21:36:19.000Z"` (Friday, after close). Of its 5 plays, MSFT and NVDA both name an options contract expiring **"Sep 28"** — the SAME calendar day the edition targets — while `dte` reports **3** for both. DELL/SPCX/VOO expire Oct 2 with `dte: 7`. A member reading this edition Monday morning and trusting the displayed `dte: 3` for MSFT/NVDA would believe they have three days of runway; the real, as-of-read remaining life on that contract is **zero** (it expires the same session). |
| **Root cause** | `pickChainContract` (`deterministic-edition.ts`) and the sibling `dte` display line (`calendarDteBetween(todayEtYmd(), contract.expiry)`) both anchor exclusively on `todayEtYmd()` — the wall-clock ET date at the moment the edition-build job actually runs — with no separate notion of "the trading day this edition is FOR" (`edition_for`) threaded through anywhere in the module; no function in the file accepts an explicit as-of date. The swing (non-day-mode) branch enforces `MIN_DTE_CALENDAR_DAYS = 2` as a `minExpiryDate(today)` floor specifically to keep "swing never trades a same-day expiry" true — but that 2-calendar-day floor is computed from the BUILD date, not the trading day the pick will actually be entered on. On an ordinary weekday-to-weekday build (built Monday evening for Tuesday) the two dates are 1 calendar day apart, well inside the buffer. On a Friday-evening build for the following Monday (the normal weekly cadence — no Saturday/Sunday sessions to publish for), the gap is 3 calendar days, which silently consumes the entire 2-day cushion: a contract expiring the very Monday the edition targets clears `expiry > today+2` (Sep 28 > Sep 25+2 = Sep 27) by exactly one day, so it is accepted as a legitimate "swing" pick even though, from the actual trading day's perspective, it has zero days of life left. |
| **Blast radius** | Both call sites inside `deterministic-edition.ts` read `todayEtYmd()` independently (`pickChainContract`'s `minExpiry`/`dayMaxExpiry` filter, and the `dte` field written into every `PlaybookPlay` at both of `buildPlay`'s two call sites — lines ~801 and ~1160). Every Legacy edition built on a Friday (or ahead of any other calendar gap wider than the intended next trading session — e.g. built the evening before a market holiday) is exposed identically: any pick whose selected contract lands in the now-collapsed cushion window reads as a normal multi-day swing pick with a materially overstated `dte`, while actually offering little-to-no real holding runway by the time the edition is read/acted on. This is a data-quality/product-correctness issue in a real, member-facing trade recommendation, not a cosmetic display bug — a member could genuinely be misled about how much time value/holding runway a "swing" pick affords. |
| **Fix rationale (not attempted in this pass)** | A correct fix needs an explicit "as-of trading day" parameter threaded from whatever determines `edition_for` (the Legacy edition-build cron/route caller) down through `buildDeterministicEditionPlays` → `buildPlay` → `pickChainContract`'s `minExpiry`/`dte` computations — replacing every internal `todayEtYmd()` read in this specific call chain with that passed-in date. That is a multi-function signature change to the core Legacy contract-selection path in a live, member-facing recommendation engine; verifying it doesn't regress the 0DTE/Vector-facing callers of the SAME shared `pickChainContract`/`buildPlay` (which correctly want same-day "today," not a future target day) needs care, plus a fixture-based regression test that reproduces a Friday-build/Monday-target scenario end-to-end. That is bigger than a one-sitting fix from a 10-minute monitoring cycle, and this file (contract selection for real trade recommendations) is not a place to rush a change without full test coverage. Raising this to the `nighthawk` OWNER lane to scope and land properly, rather than attempting a partial fix here. |
| **Evidence** | Live `GET /api/market/nighthawk/edition`, 2026-09-28: MSFT `{"dte":3,"options_play":"MSFT $515 CALL @ $4.40 — Sep 28, entry prem ~$4.40"}`, NVDA `{"dte":3,"options_play":"NVDA $225 CALL @ $1.71 — Sep 28, entry prem ~$1.71"}`, both against `edition_for: "2026-09-28"` and `published_at: "2026-09-25T21:36:19.000Z"`. `deterministic-edition.ts` source confirms: `MIN_DTE_CALENDAR_DAYS = 2` (line 232), `minExpiryDate(today)` built by adding that constant to `todayEtYmd()` (lines 234-238), consumed by the non-day-mode branch's `row.expiry < minExpiry` reject (`pickChainContract`, ~line 316), and `dte` computed the same way at both `buildPlay` call sites (`calendarDteBetween(todayEtYmd(), contract.expiry)`, lines 801 and 1160) — no `edition_for`/target-date parameter exists anywhere in `buildDeterministicEditionPlays`'s or `pickChainContract`'s signatures to anchor against instead. |
| **Status** | OPEN — root-caused and evidenced live; handed to the `nighthawk` OWNER lane rather than fixed inline (see Fix rationale). |
