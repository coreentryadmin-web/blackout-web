## 2026-09-18 — [FINDING, P3 SEO] Google Search Console had not re-crawled the sitemap in 6+ weeks — 47 of 76 live URLs were added after Google's last fetch — FIXED (resubmitted)

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **What this is** | Found during a DISCOVERY-lane SEO/GEO sweep (FULL-LIFECYCLE SCOPE EXPANSION mandate). `GET .../sites/sc-domain:blackouttrades.com/sitemaps` (Search Console API, service account `claude-seo@blackout-trades.iam.gserviceaccount.com`, per `docs/audit/CLAUDE.md` §3b) reported `lastDownloaded: 2026-08-02T19:47:48Z` and `contents: [{"submitted":"29","indexed":"0"}]` for `sitemap.xml`. The LIVE sitemap (`curl https://blackouttrades.com/sitemap.xml`) currently carries **76** `<loc>` entries — Google's own record is still counting the 29 URLs that existed 47 days ago, meaning every URL added to the site since 2026-08-02 (47 of them — `/methodology`, `/about`, most of `/learn/*`, etc., per the sitemap's own `<lastmod>` dates) has never been through a sitemap-triggered discovery pass. |
| **Corroborating signal, not proof by itself** | `searchAnalytics.query` (`dimensions: date`, last 28 days) shows real but very thin organic presence — impressions 34-81/day, clicks 0-3/day, average position improving from ~35-58 (late Aug) to ~8-17 (since ~09-02). This is consistent with the domain having *some* indexed pages already (the original 29, or ones discovered outside the sitemap via internal links/backlinks) while the 47 newer pages may simply not have had a discovery signal sent to Google at all. Not a definitive indexing failure — GSC's sitemap `indexed` count is a known-unreliable field independent of the real Index Coverage report — but the `lastDownloaded` staleness is a hard fact regardless of how that count should be read. |
| **Root cause** | Nobody had resubmitted the sitemap since 2026-08-02. Google does periodically re-fetch a submitted sitemap on its own schedule, but there's no evidence that happened here across 47 days, and nothing in this repo's cron/ops surface currently pings GSC to request a re-fetch after content changes (the sitemap generator itself, `scripts/seo/generate-marketing-dates.mjs` and friends, only regenerates the FILE — it doesn't tell Google to come get it). |
| **Fix** | `PUT .../sites/sc-domain:blackouttrades.com/sitemaps/https%3A%2F%2Fblackouttrades.com%2Fsitemap.xml` via the same service account (needs `webmasters` write scope, not just `webmasters.readonly` — the read-only scope used for `searchAnalytics.query` elsewhere in this toolkit can't submit). Returned `204`; the sitemap record immediately flipped to `isPending: true`, `lastSubmitted: 2026-09-18T21:17:45Z`. Google will re-fetch and re-derive the `submitted`/`indexed` counts on its own crawl schedule (typically within a day); this session cannot force that part synchronously. |
| **Not done / follow-up** | Did not add an automated re-submit trigger to the SEO cron surface — a one-off manual resubmit fixes today's staleness but not the recurrence; if this keeps drifting stale, the real fix is having the sitemap-generation cron (or a new lightweight one) call this same `sitemaps.submit` endpoint after it regenerates `sitemap.xml`, so freshness never depends on a sweep happening to notice it. Left as a write-up rather than built, per the "write up bigger enhancements, don't unilaterally build them" DISCOVERY-lane discipline — this is a small, clearly-scoped follow-up for the SEO lane, not something requiring architecture judgment, but it's still a new recurring cron surface someone should deliberately own. |
| **Status** | FIXED (one-time resubmit, confirmed accepted by the API). Re-check `lastDownloaded` on a future sweep to confirm Google actually re-crawled it (expect it to move off 2026-08-02); the automated-resubmit follow-up remains open. |
