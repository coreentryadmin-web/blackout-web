| **Fix rationale** | A separate, wider function (`gateFramingMootReason`) rather than widening `deadPlayReason` in place, because the two questions ("is this gate framing moot" vs. "is this play's live state permanently done") are genuinely different and `deadPlayReason`'s other two callers need the narrow answer — conflating them would have introduced a new staleness-disclosure bug while fixing a framing bug. Rejected alternative: a structural fix instead (stop treating `legacy:exempt` as a member of `commitGateBlockedBy`/`gateBlocks` at all — it's a pure provenance/disclosure tag, confirmed present even on live COMMITTED positions, never itself a blocking condition — and represent it as a separate disclosure field). That would eliminate the whole false-blocker-framing class at the root rather than special-casing each new "something else is the real blocker" scenario as it's found, but is a larger, cross-cutting change (touches the shape of `commitGateBlockedBy` itself, consumed well beyond these two render sites) — flagged as a follow-up idea for whoever wants to take it on, not done here, to keep this PR small and scoped to the one evidenced bug. |
