## 2026-08-29 — [FINDING, P2 Largo] `get_earnings` truncated on `related_news`'s full article bodies — FIXED, plus a 92-tool comprehensive re-sweep

> **kind:** `FINDING`

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Symptom** | Following up on the same-day truncation-cap work (companion PR, `2026-08-29-largo-truncation-fix-undersized-caps.md`), ran `largo-truncation-probe.mjs` across the "full coverage" tool list not touched by that PR. `get_earnings` came back `TRUNCATED` (control `get_zerodte_rejections` PROVEN in the same run), reporting `related_news` as the last key it could see. |
| **Root cause** | `run-tool.ts`'s `get_earnings` case includes `related_news: related` — up to 15 Benzinga news items from `fetchBenzingaEarnings(sym, 15)` — verbatim. `fetchBenzingaNews` (`providers/polygon.ts`) shapes each item with `body` (up to 2000 chars) and `teaser` (up to 400 chars) plus title/url/author/tickers/channels/tags. 15 such items run to ~37KB — more than double the 16,384-byte transport cap, entirely on their own, before the tool's actual primary answer (the structured earnings calendar/history/estimates, per the case's own extensive header comment) is even reached. |
| **Why this field specifically, and why it's safe to trim hard** | `related_news` is explicitly **secondary** by the code's own comment (renamed from `benzinga_news` specifically to stop it being misread as the company's own results): "stories mentioning this ticker in the earnings channel, NOT its own results — never quote it as this company's earnings" (also stated in `tool-defs.ts`'s tool description). Unlike the structured feed, nothing in the codebase depends on its `body` text — a headline-level mention (title/teaser/published/url) is all the field was ever meant to convey. |
| **Fix** | New `fitEarningsRelatedNewsForModel` (`market-data-fits.ts`) caps `related_news` to 5 items and drops `body` entirely (never needed for a headline-level mention), truncating `teaser` further to 200 chars. Wired into the `get_earnings` case via `...fittedRelatedNews`, spread after the structured fields. The pre-existing `source: ... related.length ? "benzinga_news" : ...` check still reads the FULL, un-fitted `related` array — fitting must never change what "no Benzinga news at all" means for source attribution. |
| **Regression guard** | New tests in `market-data-fits.test.ts` (3 cases): 15 real-shaped articles first prove the OLD unbounded field would exceed the cap by more than 2x on its own (sanity check against the fix going vacuous), then assert the fitted result is 5 items, `body` fully absent (not just truncated) from every item, and the whole fitted payload leaves generous headroom under the cap; plus under-cap passthrough and empty/undefined-safety cases. |
| **Comprehensive re-sweep, same session** | With this fix and the companion PR's four fixes both accounted for, ran the truncation probe across every remaining untested tool in the 129-tool catalog (three batches, ~92 tools total covered this session including the earlier companion-PR work) — **zero further truncations found**. Confirmed clean, live, 2026-08-29: `get_banger_board` (predates this session — #3026/#3045/#3046), the entire "full coverage" batch (`get_atm_chains` through `get_iv_term_structure`, `get_lotto_live` through `get_signal_log`, `get_similar_precedents` through `get_uw`), minus a handful of `INDETERMINATE` results (`get_meridian_event`, `get_option_contract`, `get_swing_discovery`, `get_technicals_spx`, `get_option_price_history`, `get_polygon`, `get_uw`) — a known probe-methodology gap, not a truncation signal: these tools need compound/specific arguments (a ticker AND an event id, a specific option contract, an explicit UW/Polygon endpoint) that a generic natural-language probe question cannot synthesize, so the model either declines or routes to a different tool, and the trace never mentions the target tool at all. Not evidence of truncation, and not chased further here — a parameterized recipe (like `get_nighthawk_dossier "ticker NVDA"` in the existing `LANE_TOOLS` list) would be needed to probe these meaningfully. |
