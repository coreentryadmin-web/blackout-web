- **What was broken:** `vexCoaching`'s (`play-brief-narrative-coaching.ts`) divergence callout compared `gammaFlip`/`vexFlip` with a FIXED DOLLAR gap — `Math.abs(gFlip - vFlip) > 0.5` — applied identically across every swing ticker regardless of underlying price, from ~$5 (RUM, GEMI) to ~$700+ (AMD). The same file's own established pattern one section up (`confluenceCoaching`, ~line 384) already normalizes a level-vs-spot gap to a PERCENT of spot for exactly this reason (`((top.center - spot) / spot) * 100`) — this bullet never got the same treatment. Live-verified against 22 real committed/watch swing positions on 2026-09-21 (AMD, HOOD, IBIT, IREN, CLSK, RIOT, APLD, AXTI, CRDO, TEM, MSTR, ABTC, BYND, MUU, SNXX, ETHU, ETHA, AMBA, FSLY, HPE, BRUN, SPCH, SNOW): **every single one where both `gammaFlip` and `vexFlip` were present tripped "diverge" — 0/22 did not.** Real gaps ranged 2.06% of spot (SNOW: gammaFlip 339.75, vexFlip 332.86) to 61.20% (SPCH: gammaFlip 10.84, vexFlip 4.50) — i.e. the $0.50 bar is so far below the real, honest gap between two independently-computed flip levels that the bullet was boilerplate: it told every trader on every ticker the same "diverge" claim regardless of whether THIS ticker's gap was unusually wide or genuinely negligible relative to its own price. This is the false-positive failure mode most visible on high-priced tickers, where even a sub-0.2%-of-spot gap (noise) would still clear the fixed $0.50 bar and read as a meaningful divergence.

Requires `spot` to compute; when `spot` is `null` the divergence claim is now silently OMITTED rather than falling back to the scale-blind absolute check, per `LARGO-PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md`'s absence principle (omission over a claim that can't be honestly scaled).
