## What today teaches the selection

**There is no ranker yet** (build order step 4), so "did the ranker pick the strongest story" has
no answer — selection was by hand all day. That is a missing component, not a scoring bug, and
filing a PR against a function that does not exist would be theatre.

What a ranker built tomorrow should carry, from today:

1. **Prefer the level that held over the level that oscillated.** IWM's 300 pin was the cleaner
   story — one number, one outcome, four cents — and it ran second in the package behind SPY's
   regime flip. SPY crossed its level three times and closed under it, which is messier to narrate
   and weaker as proof. A clean outcome is both more postable and more checkable.
2. **A structural expiry event outranks a single-name setup on the day it expires.** The BAC
   package was good; the OpEx package was the session.
3. **An outcome that can be captured from the same panel later should raise a story's score.**
   Packages whose evidence is a re-capturable panel can close their own loop; packages resting on a
   one-off moment cannot.

**Convergence check:** 5 packages covered BAC, SPY/IWM/SPX/QQQ and NVDA. No collapse onto a single
ticker. Worth re-checking once a ranker exists, since that is when convergence starts.

## Blocking the learning loop

Nothing writes queue rows. Until the `x-intel` cron or an equivalent app-side route exists, every
session's packages and outcomes are lost when the transcript ends, there is no denominator to
compute rates against, and the ranker has nothing to train on. **That is the next thing to build,
ahead of the ranker itself** — a ranker with no history to score against cannot be evaluated, and
an unevaluated ranker is just a hardcoded opinion.
